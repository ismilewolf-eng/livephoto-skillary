Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import this into iPhone Photos as a native Live Photo?
Unzip the downloaded pack in the iOS Files app. Select both IMG_LIVE.JPG and IMG_LIVE.MOV, tap the Share icon, and tap "Save to Photos". iOS automatically links both files into a single native Live Photo via the embedded Apple ContentIdentifier metadata!
Are my videos uploaded?
No. Your selected video is processed in your browser. The page loads ordinary website assets and may use aggregate analytics, but it does not upload your media file.
Which files can I use?
Try an MP4, MOV, or WebM video under 100 MB. The browser decodes the clip locally, attaches matching Apple ContentIdentifier UUIDs to the JPEG and QuickTime tracks, and packages them into an iOS-ready bundle.
