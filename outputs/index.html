Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set the exported Live Photo as my iPhone Wallpaper?
After downloading the paired ZIP, extract it in your iPhone's Files app. Select both the .JPG and .MOV files, tap Share → Save to Photos. Then go to Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper, select your Live Photo and enable "Live Photo" mode!
Are my videos uploaded to any server?
No. LivePhoto · Skillary operates 100% locally inside your browser using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and Canvas APIs. Your files never leave your device.
Why does an Apple Live Photo consist of both JPG and MOV?
Apple's Live Photo architecture requires a still image (JPG/HEIC) and a matching QuickTime video (MOV) that share the exact same ContentIdentifier UUID tag. Our engine generates both with standard Apple MakerNote Key 17 injection.
