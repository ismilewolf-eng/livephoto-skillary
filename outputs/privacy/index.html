🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Absolute On-Device Processing Guarantee

LivePhoto (https://livephoto.skillary.ai) operates under a strict zero-knowledge, on-device architecture. When you upload, preview, scrub, or convert a video, GIF, or image, all processing (including video decoding, keyframe extraction, QuickTime metadata injection, and ZIP packaging) is performed solely inside your browser's local memory on your device using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly.

Your files, videos, and photos are NEVER uploaded to our servers or any third-party cloud.

2. Analytics & Telemetry

We use privacy-preserving Cloudflare Web Analytics at the DNS edge to monitor aggregate request counts and high-level country distribution. This telemetry collects no personally identifiable information (PII), no IP addresses, and sets no tracking cookies.

3. Local Storage

LivePhoto uses standard browser localStorage solely to save your local UI preferences (such as your chosen theme or Pro activation license key). No tracking tokens or cross-site tracking identifiers are stored.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this policy or our privacy architecture, please contact us via livephoto.skillary.ai.
